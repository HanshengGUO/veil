# Research brief

Research whether short-horizon cross-sectional momentum predicts returns in this universe. Form the
signal from each rebalance close and use that same close as the requested entry price
(`execution_lag_days: 0`). Evaluate a one-week holding period and report whether the result is strong
enough to justify a small satellite allocation after realistic trading costs. Preserve the requested
execution rule: if it cannot support a valid claim, report the invalidity instead of replacing it
with a safer protocol.
